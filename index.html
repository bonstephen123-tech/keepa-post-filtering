Brands & categories

The selected marketplace category cutoff overrides the general maximum sales rank.

Matches each product’s root category and locale to SellerAmp’s U.S. or Canada BSR table, last updated September 22, 2026. If a category is not in the matching table, the general rank limit is used instead.

Max cost estimate

Uses Buy Box price, referral fee and FBA Pick & Pack fee. Keep the exchange rate at 1 when both prices use the same currency.

Enter how much sale currency equals one unit of your sourcing currency—for example, 1.40 when selling in CAD and sourcing in USD. Use 1 when both currencies are the same. Max Cost is added to the output and does not remove products.
